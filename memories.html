Memories With You

🌷 The most random beginning

Sometimes I genuinely think about how random our beginning was. We didn't meet through some dramatic conversation or some huge introduction. We literally met in a dead GC, when neither of us probably expected anything interesting to happen. You started writing the lyrics of a song, stopped halfway, and I somehow decided to complete them.

That's literally it. One unfinished lyric, one reply, and then you called me baby girl. I called you baby girl back, and somehow that tiny interaction became the beginning of everything.

💌 The first time I actually came to you

I think one of the biggest turning points was when I saw that note of yours where you were being so unnecessarily mean to yourself. You were calling yourself dumb and putting yourself down, and I remember thinking, excuse me??? Who allowed you to talk about my friend like that?

So I came into your DMs and started telling you everything I genuinely thought about you. Your confidence, your boldness, your sass, the way you carried yourself, the way you could speak your mind.

I don't even think I realised how much that conversation would matter later. We talked, I tried to make you feel better, and somewhere between that conversation and everything that followed, you stopped being just a girl from a GC and became my person to talk to.

📱 The beginning of the reel terrorism

And then came the era I was terrified of at first: sending you reels. 😭 I remember genuinely overthinking before sending them because I kept wondering whether you'd find me annoying, whether you'd even like the reel, whether I'd send too many.

And then you basically gave me permission to spam you, and that was probably the worst decision you ever made because after that there was absolutely no stopping me. 🎀

One reel became five, five became twenty, and eventually I stopped even checking how many I'd sent. It became one of those tiny things that somehow became part of our friendship.

🌙 The paragraphs we wrote while the other slept

One of the memories I think I'll always keep is our weird little routine of writing paragraphs when the other person wasn't even awake to read them.

There were nights when you would be awake while I was sleeping, writing me these huge paragraphs, and then I'd wake up and find them waiting for me. And then I'd do the exact same thing when you were asleep.

It was like we were constantly leaving pieces of our thoughts for each other to find later. 🌙

🫧 The day I forced you to get that injection

I don't think I'll ever forget the day you were ill and I basically turned into your extremely annoying unpaid doctor. You didn't want to get the injection, and I kept forcing you to do it because apparently I had suddenly decided that your health was my responsibility. 😭

And then, as if I hadn't already annoyed you enough, you didn't talk to me for basically the entire day.

Looking back, it's honestly one of those memories that makes me smile because it was so ridiculously us.

🪻 The jealousy era

And obviously, we cannot talk about our memories without talking about my jealousy era. There were definitely moments when I'd see you talking to someone else and suddenly I'd be sitting there like, oh, okay, interesting. 😭

I wouldn't always say anything directly, but somewhere inside my head I'd already started creating an entire courtroom case.

Looking back, it's honestly hilarious because half the time there wasn't even anything serious happening. It was just me being me, getting unnecessarily jealous and then pretending I wasn't jealous at all.

🎀 The ridiculous fights that weren't really fights

We've had those moments where something tiny would turn into an argument, and five minutes later neither of us even remembered why we were annoyed.

Sometimes you'd say something, I'd take it the wrong way, you'd explain yourself, I'd explain myself, and suddenly we were writing paragraphs again.

And somehow even our arguments became memories.

📖 The poem that actually got me

And then there was that poem. I genuinely don't think you realise how much that one stayed with me.

You wrote this huge poem for me, and somewhere between reading all those words and realising how much thought you'd put into them, you managed to completely win my heart.

There are certain words people say that disappear after a few days, and then there are words that somehow stay. Yours stayed. 💌

🕊️ All the little versions of us

When I think about our friendship, I don't actually think about one single huge moment. I think about hundreds of tiny versions of us.

The GC version of us. The DM version. The late-night version. The jealous version. The annoyed version. The reel-spamming version. The paragraph-writing version. The version where one of us was upset and the other tried to fix it.

Somehow all those tiny moments became something much bigger than either of us expected.

🌙 And somehow, here we are

It's funny because when we first met, I had absolutely no idea that I'd eventually know so many little things about you.

I don't know what the future is going to look like, and I don't know how many more memories we're going to make, but I know that the ones we've already made will always have a little place in my heart.

From one unfinished lyric in a dead GC to this much history between us... I think that's a pretty beautiful accident. 🌷
