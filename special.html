🕊️ How Important You Are to Me

A little something I wanted you to know. 🌷

I don't think I've ever properly explained how important you actually are to me. Not in the dramatic “you're my whole world” way, but in the little everyday way. You're one of those people whose presence somehow became a normal part of my life without me even noticing when it happened. There are things I automatically want to tell you, random thoughts that somehow feel incomplete if I don't share them with you, and moments where I'll see something and immediately think, Aisha would understand this.

You're important to me because I don't have to make myself into someone else around you. I can be stupid, annoying, quiet, overly excited, confused, dramatic, or just completely random, and somehow you're still there. And I think that's something I value more than I can properly explain. It's not about talking every second or constantly proving how much we care about each other. It's simply knowing that there's a person somewhere who has become a familiar part of my life.

You've also become one of those people whose happiness genuinely matters to me. When you're happy, I want to hear about it. When something good happens to you, I want to celebrate it with you. And when you're having a horrible day, even if I don't always know exactly what to say, a part of me still wants to make things a little easier for you. I don't need some huge reason for that. You're important to me because you're you.

And maybe that's why our friendship means so much to me. I don't know exactly what we'll look like years from now, or where life will take either of us, but I know that meeting you has already given me memories, feelings and a friendship that I wouldn't want to erase. Some people enter your life loudly and disappear just as quickly. And then there are people who quietly become a part of your life until one day you realise, “Wait… when did this person become this important to me?” You're that person for me. 🌷
