How Important You Are To Me

I don't think I can properly put into words what you mean to me, because you're not important to me for just one particular reason. It's the person you are as a whole. Your confidence, your honesty, your dramatic little reactions, your stubbornness, your softness, your ability to make even the most ordinary conversation feel entertaining, and even those parts of you that you sometimes don't see in yourself.

You have this very distinct personality that is impossible to ignore, and somehow, knowing you has made you one of those people whose presence genuinely matters to me. I care about what you think, what makes you happy, what makes you upset, and even the completely random things happening in your life that probably wouldn't matter to anyone else.

I want you to know that you never have to be perfect for me to value you. You can be confusing, annoying, overly dramatic, stubborn, emotional, loud, quiet, or simply have one of those days where you don't feel like yourself, and none of that makes you any less worthy of being cared about. 🌷

I think what makes you so special to me is that you're you. There isn't some perfect version of you that I expect you to become before I'll care about you.

I genuinely like the person underneath all the confidence and chaos—the girl who can be incredibly soft, who cares deeply even when she pretends she doesn't, who has so much going on inside her head, and who sometimes needs someone to remind her of her own worth.

I may not always know exactly what to say, and I definitely won't always understand everything you do, but that doesn't change the fact that I want good things for you.

I want you to laugh without forcing it, feel proud of yourself without needing someone else's approval, and eventually look at yourself with the same kindness that other people can see in you. 💌

And if there's one thing I want you to take from this entire thing, it's that you matter to me simply because you're Aisha.

Not because of what you can give me, not because of how much attention you give me, and not because you have to constantly prove anything.

You're a person I genuinely value, and that's something I don't say lightly.

No matter how chaotic, ridiculous, stubborn, dramatic, or completely impossible you can be sometimes, there's still a very special place in my heart that belongs to you.

And honestly, I hope you never underestimate how loved and appreciated you can be by the people who genuinely know you. 🪻
